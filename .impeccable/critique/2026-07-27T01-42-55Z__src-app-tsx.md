  - **Why it matters**: Screen readers cannot associate the question heading with option choices.
  - **Fix**: Wrap option choices in a `<fieldset>` with an accessible `<legend className="sr-only">`.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden src/components/Assessment.tsx`

- **[P2] What**: Contact form relies on native `<select>` dropdown for service selection.
  - **Why it matters**: Hides service capabilities behind a dropdown, increasing cognitive load for C-suite visitors.
  - **Fix**: Replace native `<select>` with clickable, visual option card selectors for "Layanan yang Dibutuhkan".
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout src/components/Contact.tsx`
