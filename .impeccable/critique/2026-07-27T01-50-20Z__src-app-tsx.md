#### Priority Issues

- **[P2] What:** `Ctrl + /` keyboard shortcut for AI Advisor is unindicated in the UI.
  - **Why it matters:** Power users (Audit Heads) won't discover the shortcut naturally.
  - **Fix:** Add a visible `<kbd>` badge (`Ctrl+/`) inside the Header and Hero CTA buttons.
  - **Suggested command:** `$impeccable delight`

- **[P2] What:** Acronym tooltips (`GlossaryTooltip`) near viewport edges on mobile screens may clip.
  - **Why it matters:** Mobile compliance reviewers may miss acronym definitions on narrow viewports.
  - **Fix:** Apply viewport edge collision clamping and `max-w-[85vw]` to tooltip containers.
  - **Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

- **[P3] What:** Absence of an explicit "Lewati Pertanyaan" (Skip Question) option in the Assessment wizard.
  - **Why it matters:** Users unsure about a specific COSO dimension may feel stuck or guess inaccurate scores.
  - **Fix:** Add a subtle `"Lewati Pertanyaan"` option that advances the step while defaulting score to level 1.
  - **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

- **[P3] What:** Focus state isn't explicitly set when stepping backward in the 2-step Contact form.
  - **Why it matters:** Screen reader and keyboard users experience focus loss when clicking "Kembali".
  - **Fix:** Call `inputRef.current?.focus()` on step navigation.
  - **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`
