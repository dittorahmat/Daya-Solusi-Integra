#### Overall Impression
Daya Solusi Integra presents a state-of-the-art, institutional dark-mode experience that balances corporate authority for BUMN executives with high-utility interactive GRC tools (COSO Self-Assessment wizard and ambient AI Consultant drawer). The primary opportunity for improvement lies in subtle micro-UX refinements: segmenting the 10-item service selection grid in `Contact.tsx` and adding a direct jump step-bar in the assessment wizard.

#### What's Working
1. **Uncompromised BUMN Visual Authority**: Dark navy canvas with BUMN Blue and Gold accents conveys state-level consulting authority without falling into generic SaaS tropes.
2. **Context-Aware Data Continuity**: Seamless state bridge pre-filling assessment organization name and sector directly into the consultation intake form.
3. **Board-Ready Executive Output**: Native `@media print` styling formats the interactive dashboard into a clean, printable white-paper report for board and audit committee meetings.

#### Priority Issues
- **[P2] What**: Choice overload in service interest selection grid (`Contact.tsx`)
  - **Why it matters**: 10 unsegmented checkbox options in a 2-column grid increase visual scan time for executives seeking specific advisory services.
  - **Fix**: Group services into 2 logical categories: *Tata Kelola & Kepatuhan (GRC/ICOFR)* and *Keamanan & Kontrol TI (ITGC/ISO)*.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout`
- **[P2] What**: Absence of direct step jump navigation in Assessment wizard (`Assessment.tsx`)
  - **Why it matters**: Users on Question 8 wanting to revise Question 2 must click the back button 6 times sequentially.
  - **Fix**: Add a compact step indicator pill navigation bar (1..10) enabling direct jumps to previously answered questions.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout`
- **[P3] What**: Mobile header drawer CTA button vertical spacing on small viewports (`Header.tsx`)
  - **Why it matters**: High padding on mobile drawer CTA buttons can push secondary nav links off-screen on compact screens (<375px).
  - **Fix**: Adjust button padding from `py-3` to `py-2.5` and optimize mobile text size.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable adapt`
