#### Overall Impression
Daya Solusi Integra presents a state-of-the-art, institutional dark-mode experience that balances corporate authority for BUMN executives with high-utility interactive GRC tools (COSO Self-Assessment wizard and ambient AI Consultant drawer).

#### What's Working
1. **Uncompromised BUMN Visual Authority**: Dark navy canvas with BUMN Blue and Gold accents conveys state-level consulting authority without falling into generic SaaS tropes or hacker neon themes.
2. **Categorized Service Discovery**: Grouping services into *Tata Kelola, Risiko & Kepatuhan* and *Keamanan & Audit Kontrol TI* reduces choice overload.

- **[P3] Numeric Hotkey Auto-Advance**: In `Assessment.tsx`, pressing `1-4` selects the option and advances after 320ms. While fast for power users, an accidental keypress advances the step.
